PRIVACY POLICY

Your files.
Your control.

Revised October 7, 2026

Access and use

The application accesses file contents and metadata for owner-directed file operations. Its granted permissions can include reading, creating, modifying, and deleting files.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed through Google Drive and the owner’s devices or selected storage destinations. Local files and cached copies may remain until removed. This website does not receive Drive files or authorization credentials.

Protection

Google OAuth controls account access. Credentials are stored in the owner’s rclone environment; their protection depends on the device’s access controls.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls retained copies and can delete them from the relevant devices or services. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not erase existing copies.

Revocation

Access can be removed through Google Account’s third-party connections settings.

Limited Use

Fleur Noire Drive Sync’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
